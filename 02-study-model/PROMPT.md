# Starter prompt · Card Model to Real House

The prompt I would start with to rebuild this demo. It won't give you my page. It gets you a first version of the idea, and then the real work starts.

Swap the parts in `[brackets]` for your own subject.

```text
I want to build a scroll-driven landing page for [an architecture studio, your subject].

The idea: a white card study model of [a house on a slope] sits on an architect's desk at
night. Card walls, acetate windows, greyboard contour layers for the land, card trees and
one tiny scale figure. A desk lamp lights it, and drawings of the same house are pinned on
the dark wall behind.

The scroll, in order:
1. The loader: a scalpel scores a line across a sheet of card, then the sheet parts along
   the cut.
2. The hero builds itself: the lamp clicks on, the contour layers drop in, the house slots
   into its site, the trees stand up.
3. The dive. The camera comes down to the scale figure's eye height. Every halving of the
   height costs the same amount of scroll. A readout shows the height in centimetres on
   the desk and in metres in the house.
4. The turn. A sphere grows from the lamp's bulb. Inside it the card becomes concrete,
   oak, glass and grass, with a thin amber scan line where it cuts each surface. The
   camera takes the figure's place, and the lamp's light becomes a low sun from the same
   direction. The sphere stops once the house and its land are done, and the dark studio
   stays around them. No sky.
5. The walk: through the sliding door into [the living room], up to [the study], where the
   same card model sits on the desk.
6. Back out. The sphere shrinks, the house turns back into card, the lamp clicks off, and
   [Start a project] comes in.

What I care about:
- The model is not a picture. 1 unit is 1 m of house, the desk is built 100 times bigger
  around it, and one parts list renders either as card or as real materials, at the same
  coordinates.
- Card and house are lit separately: the card by the lamp, the house by the sun.
- The look: a near black page, cream card #f2efe7, one amber accent #e85d04, Chakra Petch
  for headlines, IBM Plex Mono for labels, corner registration marks.
- three.js 0.160 from a CDN, plain files, no build step. No image files, every texture is
  drawn in code.

Before you write any code, propose the house with sizes I can picture and the camera path
stop by stop, and wait for my answer. Then build in checkpoints. First checkpoint only:
the loader, the hero and the dive to the figure. Record it and show me before you go
further.
```

The last paragraph matters as much as the idea. Without it you get a finished page you did not choose.
