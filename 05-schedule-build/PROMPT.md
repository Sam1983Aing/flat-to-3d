# Starter prompt · Schedule Build

The prompt I would start with to rebuild this demo. It won't give you my page. It gets you a first version of the idea, and then the real work starts.

Swap the parts in `[brackets]` for your own subject.

```text
I want to build a landing page for [a design and build company, your subject] that
promises "Your house in [14 weeks]."

The idea: a construction programme, the Gantt chart builders work from, sits in a
full-width dock along the bottom of the screen: hatched bars, the weeks along the top, an
orange "today" line. Above it is the site, still empty. The mouse wheel moves the today
line, and the house builds itself in construction order: [fence and cabin, dig,
foundations, timber frames, floors, walls, roof, windows, cladding and finishes,
furniture]. Scroll back and it comes apart again.

What the visitor sees, in order:
1. Site set-up and the dig: a fence goes up, a digger digs the pit.
2. A crane lowers each frame, wall and roof panel into place, with a little sway and a
   soft landing.
3. The cladding goes on, the lawn grows, the lights come on inside.
4. At handover the site kit leaves, the front door swings open, and the camera walks in
   through [the entry, the living room, the kitchen, up the stair to the loft], ending on
   [the gallery over the double height].

What I care about:
- The chart is not a picture. One table of phases (name, start week, end week) draws the
  bars and decides when each part of the house exists. Every part knows its phase and its
  order in it.
- The live phase is orange in both places: its bar fills, and the piece being placed gets
  orange edges.
- The camera holds one high three-quarter view during the build, so the building is what
  moves. Dragging the chart scrubs it too, and a play button runs it.
- Not cartoonish: concrete, glulam, OSB, charred larch, zinc and oak, textured in code,
  soft shadows, a sun that climbs with the weeks, faint ink edges. Dates count working
  days.
- The look: paper #f2efe7, ink #1d1c19, one orange #e85d04, Chakra Petch for headlines,
  IBM Plex Mono for labels, CAD corner marks, and a dark readout with the week, the date
  and the phase.
- three.js 0.169 from a CDN, plain files, no build step. No image files.

Before you write any code, propose the house with sizes, the phases with their weeks, and
the camera for the build and for the walk inside, and wait for my answer. Then build in
checkpoints. First checkpoint only: the dock with the programme, the site, and the build
up to the frames. Record it and show me before you go further.
```

The last paragraph matters as much as the idea. Without it you get a finished page you did not choose.
